Scope of work

Full-stack security, weighted toward DevSecOps.

DevSecOps & secure SDLC

shift-left · security gates · secure coding

Embedding security controls into the development lifecycle: SAST, dependency scanning, secret scanning and infrastructure config checks running automatically in CI/CD, with gates tiered by severity.

Monitoring & detection

SIEM · infrastructure metrics · detection engineering

Operating a unified view of security alerts and performance metrics, writing detection rules, cutting alert noise and standardising the investigation workflow.

Response & automation

SOAR · threat intel · runbooks

Automating alert triage and enrichment to shorten response time; writing operational runbooks and incident handling procedures.

Infrastructure & cloud hardening

web-facing services · IAM · secrets

Tightening configuration of internet-facing systems; reviewing cloud permissions against least privilege, centralising secrets management, separating environments.

Training & documentation

secure coding · threat modeling

Building curricula and teaching development teams: OWASP Top 10, threat modeling (STRIDE), thinking in abuse cases. Writing internal standards and documentation.
